Philosophy

0DTE removes overnight risk but increases intraday gamma. Success comes from aligning strategy with risk tolerance and executing it mechanically.

Three pillars: strikes · risk · execution

Core Rules

  • Stop trading for the day at -2% account P&L.
  • Stops: 3× credit on the short leg only (market order).
  • Close all 0DTE positions by 3:45 PM ET.

When to Stand Down

  • VIX > 30 or spikes > 15% intraday.
  • Major news within 2 hours (FOMC, CPI, NFP, Fed chair).
  • Emotional, distracted, or after hitting daily loss.

Pre-Market Preparation Protocol (15 minutes)
  1. Economic calendar: note high-impact events; avoid 1 hour before/after FOMC, CPI, NFP, Fed chair.
  2. /ES futures: >1% move = trend day → Gap Hunter only; <0.3% = neutral, prefer condors.
  3. VIX:
    • <15: low premium; consider 10–15 delta, iron condors.
    • 15–20: sweet spot; standard rules.
    • 20–30: rich premium; 5–10 delta further OTM; delay entries.
    • >30: do not trade.
  4. SPY top holdings: weight green vs red to set bias (strong bullish >+20, strong bearish <-20).
  5. Daily implied move: anchor strike boundaries at ± expected move (e.g., ATM straddle × 0.6 ÷ 2).
  6. Set intention: choose bias, strategy (PUT/CALL/IC/Gap Hunter), entry window, and position size.
Directional Bias Decision Matrix

🟢 Bullish → Sell PUT Spreads

  • VIX declining >3% or in contango.
  • SPY holdings 7+ green or weighted score >+15.
  • SPX above prior close; internals supportive (ADD >+500, TICK >+200).

🔴 Bearish → Sell CALL Spreads

  • VIX rising >3% or backwardation.
  • SPY holdings 7+ red or score <-15.
  • SPX below prior close; internals weak (ADD <-500, TICK <-200).
⚪ Neutral → Iron Condor when VIX flat (<20), mixed holdings, tight range (<0.3% after 30 min) and prior day range <0.8%.
✨ Gap Hunter: use only on ±1% gaps; wait for midday support/resistance, sell 10-delta spread beyond that level, set GTC at 30% profit.
Three-Method Strike Validation

Method 1: Delta (Primary)

  • Low VIX: 10–15 delta; Normal: 8–12; High: 5–10; >30 VIX: skip.
  • Example at SPX 5,500: low VIX PUT 5,440 (12Δ); normal PUT 5,430 (8Δ); high PUT 5,410 (5Δ).

Method 2: Implied Move

  • Place shorts at/ beyond ± expected move boundary.
  • If delta strike is inside boundary → wait/skip.
  • Example: SPX 5,500, move ±20 → PUT ≤5,480, CALL ≥5,520.

Method 3: Volume by Strike

  • Identify Max Put (support) and Max Call (resistance) volume on 0DTE chain.
  • PUT short at/below Max Put; CALL short at/above Max Call.
Trade only when all three align (high probability). If only two align → half size; one → no trade.
Spread Width Strategy

Standard (90%): 10-point

  • Manageable $1,000 max loss/contract; good premium (15–25% of width).
  • Default for normal conditions and clearer setups.

Alternatives

  • 5-point: faster theta, faster to max loss; use when credit >15% width.
  • 20–25 point: use rarely (10% of trades) in high VIX or Gap Hunter with conviction; behaves closer to naked.
Never sell for <15% of width. Example minimums: 5-pt ≥$0.75; 10-pt ≥$1.50; 20-pt ≥$3.00.
Position Sizing – The Survival Formula
  1. Max daily loss = 2% of account. Example $50k → $1,000.
  2. Decide trades/day (1–2). Max loss per trade = daily max ÷ trades.
  3. Max credit per trade = per-trade loss ÷ 2 (stop ~2× credit).
  4. Contracts = max credit ÷ credit per contract.
  5. Verify buying power: contracts × width × $100 ≤ 50% of account.

Examples (10-pt)

  • $25k: max loss $500 → max credit $500 → 2 contracts @ $2.50 (BP $20k).
  • $50k: max loss $1k → 4 contracts @ $2.50 (BP $40k).
Iron condor counts as one trade; credit = both sides. Adjust down if BP exceeds 50%.
Entry Timing & Execution

Windows

  • Early (9:40–9:45 ET): capture higher IV; use when bias clear, VIX <18.
  • Delayed (10:00–10:30): for high VIX, gaps >0.5%, mixed signals.
  • Never enter after 3:30 PM; close by 3:45 PM.

Order Flow

  • Use limit entries near mid; adjust by $0.05 if no fill.
  • Beginners: enter iron condor as one order; advanced traders can leg.
  • Confirm fill, recalc stops, set alerts within 2 minutes.
Stop Loss Strategy (Non-Negotiable)

Primary Rule

  • Stop triggers when short leg premium = 3× initial credit (market order).
  • Net loss ≈ 2× credit; prevents naked risk when long leg decays.

Placement

  • Stop on short leg only; full-spread stops can fail late day.
  • Iron condor: separate stops per side using each side's credit.
  • Time overrides: down 100% by noon or 150% by 2 PM → consider exit.
Use market stops for certainty; slippage is acceptable versus unlimited risk. VIX spike >15% intraday → flatten immediately.
Profit Targets & Management
  • Directional spreads: target 60–75% profit; often 2–4 hours.
  • Iron condors: 50% profit or $0.10–$0.20 remaining; close winner side early.
  • Use GTC to close at target; ratchet stop to breakeven after 50% profit (advanced).
Close everything by 3:45 PM. Gap Hunter: GTC at 30% profit immediately after entry.
Managing Stopped Positions

After Stop Hits

  • Breathe; log loss; reassess remaining daily risk.
  • Evaluate long leg value—can offset or exceed loss in strong trend.
  • Wait 15–30 minutes before any re-entry; require fresh alignment.

Re-Entry Rules

  • Use same delta at new, farther OTM strike; reduce size.
  • Iron condor with one side stopped: close all if near loss limit or after 2 PM; otherwise manage remaining side cautiously.
Advanced Tactics & Market Adaptation

VIX-Based Adjustments

  • Low VIX: 12–15 delta, prefer iron condors, anchor to prior high/low, consider 1–2 DTE.
  • Normal: standard 8–12 delta.
  • High (20–30): 5–8 delta, delay entries, consider wider spreads; close profits early.
  • Very High (>30): do not trade 0DTE.

Gap & Breadth Signals

  • Small gap <0.7%: normal rules.
  • Medium 0.7–1.5%: trend continuation bias; tighten deltas.
  • Large >1.5%: Gap Hunter only.
  • Internals alignment strengthens conviction; divergence can signal fades.
SPY holdings give leadership signal: tech-heavy weakness can justify CALL spreads even if SPX is green; inverse logic for dips.
